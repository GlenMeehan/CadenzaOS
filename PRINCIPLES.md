Principles

Cadenza is built on the idea that progress should be good for everyone it touches. In practice:

1. People’s data and privacy come first. Cadenza does not collect, transmit or hide data about its users. Anything the system learns or records (command history, telemetry, predictions) is stored locally, scoped to the user it belongs to, and can be inspected and deleted by that user.

2. No hidden behaviour. Features that observe or predict are visible, documented and optional. Models are inspectable as plain data.

3. Suggestions, not actions. The system may suggest, but never takes irreversible or destructive actions on a user’s behalf without explicit confirmation.

4. Contributions benefit everyone, not only the contributor. A change should not trade away other people’s rights, safety or security for one person’s or one organisation’s gain.

5. Credit is shared fairly. Contributors keep authorship of their work, and it is credited.

6. Help is given and asked for freely. Questions are welcome at every level, and reviews explain the reasoning, not only the verdict.
